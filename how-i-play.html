General tips

How I play Camille

Short, clean trades in lane, then push and roam to put pressure on the whole map.

01Trading pattern

  • Into most champions

    Q a minion, use the move speed to W the enemy, then Q2 auto for the true damage and leave.

  • Play around your passive

    Trade when the shield is ready and back off while it's on cooldown. Since 26.16 it's smaller early but back every 14s, so look for more short trades rather than long ones.

  • Passive timing trick

    E in just before your passive is back up. It comes off cooldown while they're stunned, so the shield is fresh for when they hit back instead of being wasted.

  • Buffer CC

    If you're mid Q auto when their CC lands, the Q auto still goes through. Press E just before you get CC'd to cancel it and still move, but once E1 starts pulling you to the wall, they can interrupt the dash. Q is especially handy into Riven, Renekton and Pantheon, and it works on any stun.

  • Land the W edge

    The outer cone slows and heals you; the center doesn't.

  • Stormraider's lanes

    A different trade. Dump as much damage as possible to proc it, even using E to get in, then use the move speed to get out before they trade back. Repeat every time your cooldowns are up. You can play much more aggressively and get away with E starts, especially when their Bone Plating is down.

02Push and roam

  • Shove, then move

    Push the wave in and look for plays mid or in the river.

  • Slow push before you back

    So you lose nothing while shopping.

  • Crash for plates

    Once you have Sheen, since Q2 true damage burns turrets fast.

  • Proxy with Hydra

    Rushing Ravenous Hydra lets you proxy and play near their tower, then sustain back up off the wave.

  • Hold it in bad lanes

    Keep the wave near your tower and farm with Q until you can shove.

03The vision game

  • Leave your side lane

    Disappear from vision: just the threat that you might show up mid pressures their whole team.

  • Group for objectives

    Be with your team before Baron or Dragon soul.

04Teamfight role

  • Assassin-diver

    E from fog onto their carry, then R to cut off peel.

  • R to isolate

    It knocks back everyone else, so use it to cut the target off rather than as damage.

  • Wait for their CC

    Let key crowd control (displacements, stuns) go out before you commit.

  • Flank

    E in from an angle; E-ing in from the front gets you peeled.
